Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version: NameLoom generates names entirely in your browser. We collect no personal information through the tool itself. We show ads and use analytics — both use cookies you can control.

Who we are

NameLoom is a free random name generator operated as an independent website. For privacy enquiries: hello@nameloom.net

What we do NOT collect

The name generation on NameLoom works entirely within your browser using JavaScript. No information about which names you generate, which categories you select, or any preferences you set is ever transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere.

What we do collect

  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — which categories are most popular, traffic sources, and session duration. This data is aggregated and anonymous.
  • Google AdSense: We display advertisements served by Google. Google uses cookies to personalise ads based on your browsing history. This is governed by Google's own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare logs: Our hosting provider automatically records standard server logs including IP addresses, retained briefly for security purposes.

Cookies

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics): Opt out at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Advertising cookies (Google AdSense): Manage at adssettings.google.com

Affiliate links

NameLoom contains affiliate links to third-party services (including D&D Beyond, Amazon, Namecheap, and Etsy). These are clearly marked as sponsored. If you click and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no cost to you. We are not responsible for third-party privacy practices.

Your rights under UK GDPR

If you are in the UK or EU, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or port data we hold about you, and to object to processing. As we collect minimal data (primarily via cookies you can control), most rights are exercisable through your browser settings. Contact hello@nameloom.net for any other requests.

Children

NameLoom is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The tool collects no personal data whatsoever during use.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The date above reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the site constitutes acceptance.

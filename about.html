About NameLoom

What is NameLoom?

NameLoom is a free random name generator with eight distinct categories, built for writers, gamers, parents, founders, and anyone who needs the perfect name. Every generation happens instantly in your browser — no account required, no data collected, unlimited use.

Names are drawn from carefully curated lists built around each category's conventions — phonetic patterns for fantasy races, etymological research for baby names, and branding principles for business names. We don't use AI generation — every name has been hand-selected for quality and authenticity.

Can I use these names commercially?

Yes. All names generated by NameLoom are free to use for any purpose — published fiction, games, business names, characters and more. Names themselves are not subject to copyright. If a generated name happens to match an existing trademark, check clearance before commercial use.

Privacy and your data

All name generation happens entirely in your browser. No information about which names you generate, which categories you select, or any other usage data is transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere. We display advertising to keep the site free — see our Privacy Policy for full details.
